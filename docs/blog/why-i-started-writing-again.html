Why I Started Writing Again

August 15, 2026 · 3 min read · Sarthak Kulshrestha · Stories

  • Writing
  • Reflection

A seed post for the stories section — placeholder prose I'll replace with the real thing.

This is the first thing I've published here in a while. The blog didn't stop on purpose. It just quietly slid down the list until enough time had passed that starting again felt heavier than it should have.

Why it went quiet

It is easy to blame being busy, and being busy was part of it. But the truer reason is that somewhere along the way I started treating a post as something that had to be complete, defensible, and unquestionably worth a stranger's time before it could go up. That is an efficient way to never publish anything. Every draft looked thin next to the fuller version of it in my head, so every draft stayed a draft.

What changed

A few small things. I reread some old posts I had half-decided to delete, and they were fine — useful, even, mostly to the person who wrote them. I also noticed how often I was working out a problem I had already solved once before, because the solution had only ever lived in my memory. Writing it down a single time would have paid for itself several times over.

And I think I got tired of the standard. Not lowering it exactly, but moving it: from "is this the definitive piece on the subject" to "is this true, and would it have helped me a year ago."

What this space is for now

Notes, mostly. Working through something and leaving a trail for the next person — often that next person is a future version of me. Some of it will be technical. Some of it won't. Occasionally it will just be what I saw when I pointed a telescope at the sky. I am dropping the requirement that a post feel finished before it gets published.

Closing thought

If you write and you have stalled, the advice I would give myself is simple: lower the bar. A short, honest, slightly incomplete post that actually exists beats a comprehensive one that never does. That is the entire reason this page isn't blank anymore.
